# Fine detail lives in shading, driven by Surface knowledge, not in geometry

OSDS generates the whole US at runtime, so it cannot model fine detail (puddles, cracks, grime, moss, dirt, wear, imperfections) as polygons: the cost and coverage would be impossible. The 3D layer stays simple: roads, ground, buildings and objects at modest polygon counts. Fine detail is added in shading by layered materials that follow a Surface knowledge table: rules about where real surfaces age and weather, evaluated from cheap inputs the shader already has (height above ground, distance across the road, curvature, facing, climate, weather, land use, a stable per-object seed). The CPU AI may precompute per-Tile masks (for example, where puddles, wear and cracks really are) into the World cache; it never runs per frame. Parallax, detail normals, decals and grass shells give apparent depth without geometry.
