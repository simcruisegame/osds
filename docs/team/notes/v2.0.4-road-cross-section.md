# v2.0.4: road cross-section and levels

Work held back from v2.0.3, which keeps every height and only leaves out bridge faces that stand inside another carriageway.

1. **Decks clear the roads they cross.** A deck over a carriageway rises until its underside clears it by 4.9 m, with smoothstep ramps that add 6 % grade at most and stay off the ribbon's ends. The code is on branch `v2.0.4-bridge-lift` (`FOsdsRoadNetwork::Build`, step 4b). Without embankment terrain the ramps float, so it waits for item 3.
2. **Roads under a bridge go into a cut.** The counterpart of item 1, for where the map puts the lower road in a cutting or at a lower `layer`: Fisher Freeway (I-75) under Woodward Avenue in Detroit, I-285 under Northcrest Road in Doraville. The tile data must first carry OSM `layer`, `tunnel` and `cutting` (helper). The crossing record `FOsdsRoadCrossing` (release-6) lists the crossings and their clearance.
3. **Embankments and cuts in the terrain** for both: road conditioning shapes the ground to a lifted or lowered road with real side slopes.
4. **A recovery width before a cut slope (P3).** Where the road edge meets a steep cut slope, the ground rises straight from the asphalt edge, with no shoulder or verge. A car that runs wide hits a bank, not a slope. At Bixby south (CA-1, 36.3693-36.3705) the drive robot took a tight left curve too fast and stuck on the slope (release re-test of c1a6b76: `final-c1a6b76\owner-1930\bixby-south\frame-12.0.png`, `frame-13.0.png`; jolt 113.8 m/s^2 at 200 m). Rule: keep the ground no higher than the road edge out to 2-3 m (the recovery width), then the cut slope.
5. **Junction lips** (Welch, WV and Doraville, GA; branch `v2.0.4-junction-lips`).
