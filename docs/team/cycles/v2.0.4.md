## Playtest triage and recommended order (15 November 2026)

Baseline reviewed: `c26ed5c` (the v2.0.3 merge). Put the reported driveability failures ahead of the experimental graphics and AI work. The items below refine the proposed objectives; they do not change the team's release scope until accepted at the next meet.

### P0: make driving reliable

1. **Manual-shift reliability (Car / Engineering).** Buffer a shift long enough for the same-step clutch ramp to reach the 0.75 engagement threshold. Use the same buffered path for live input and physics-step replay, while keeping the reverse-at-speed and over-rev guards. Done when the movement-interface test covers clutchless refusal and clutch-plus-shift in one frame, and the manual launch/shift benches stay in band.
2. **Bridge profile continuity and clearance (World / QA).** Establish a deterministic profile across the whole bridge chain and share its endpoint height across tile seams. Bridge seams must follow the deck profile rather than natural ground underneath it. Apply clearance and at-grade decisions to the shared chain; do not lift or de-deck just one tile of a continuing span. Done when tests cover spans with two, one, or no local abutments, both tile build orders, and multi-tile seams; adjacent profiles agree, the deck clears mapped roads by at least 4.9 m where required, and both-direction review drives show no gap, lip, impact, or vehicle upset. Update `scripts/bridge-check.ps1` to include Doraville and a tile-seam overpass, and to distinguish allowed clearance changes from regressions at Bixby Bridge.
3. **Road-under-ground and handling replay gate (Car / World / QA).** Reproduce wandering with the input recorder before changing steering or grip coefficients. Capture steering command and rack angle, yaw and lateral speed, and each wheel's contact, normal, surface, grip, load, slip, and force on a flat straight and the reported road. The ground-over-road probe is currently only a report: on the recorded Doraville fixture it found 56 points over 0.1 m across 9 tiles (worst 0.98 m; 4 at seams). Make the recorded-place check a release gate over the full configured place set and drive those points to zero above the 0.1 m tolerance, including the Doraville approach and junction lips. A repeated input tape must reproduce the same cross-track and yaw trace.
4. **Finish the surface-grip data path (Car).** The baseline already uses TMeasy tire curves and collision-face grip multipliers: dry asphalt/concrete 1.0, gravel 0.7, and grass 0.8; at full wetness these are 0.70, 0.63, and 0.648 respectively. Snow and ice are not mapped yet. Keep tire-force behavior separate from the surface multiplier. Add sourced snow/ice values and integration checks from collision face through per-wheel forces, including wet surfaces and a tire straddling two materials. Keep the current wet values as a reference until measurements justify a change.

### P1: make the first drive easier to understand

5. **Controller-first UI (Design / QA).** Finish the planned US-biased search and “Surprise me” road selection, then add one graphics-quality selector in Settings (`Auto`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Ultra`) with a reset-to-Auto option. Done when all choices are reachable with a pad, focus and input hints stay visible after device switches, and the graphics choice persists. Keep the change to the discovery flow and one quality control rather than a broad menu rewrite.

6. **Buildings that follow their records (World / Picture).** Use mapped roof forms when the tile's place graph has a reliable `roof:shape`; keep a procedural fallback for unknown tags. Follow with terrain-aware foundations and measured facade/roof finishes only after footprint provenance and visual checks are in place. Done when representative flat, gabled, and hipped buildings match their records and do not float or sink on sloping terrain.

## Local progress on the 2.0.4 candidate

The checkout is based on `c26ed5c` and remains uncommitted. Vehicle, bridge, and UI/graphics reviews were split into three team subtasks. The first fixes in the local tree are buffered manual shifting (live and input-tape paths), consuming the helper's full-chain bridge deck profile (tested without a local abutment and in both seam build orders), and honoring supported mapped residential roof forms. The targeted roof and bridge regressions pass. `scripts/check.ps1 -Tier full` passed 234 tests with no logged errors; its visual gate passed Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue, and correctly failed the intentionally empty TestPad fixture. The terrain-over-road probe still reports 56 points above its 0.1 m threshold across 9 Doraville tiles (worst 0.98 m), so terrain conditioning remains an open gate rather than a claimed fix.

Keep the planned ground-relative atmosphere, camera rebase, and wet-road audio work behind these reliability gates. Defer the distilled eye and process-true corridor texture experiments unless the drive, bridge, random-place, and visual-budget gates pass first. Existing world work already includes wall materials, doors, windows, and aerial-sampled tree color; do not spend 2.0.4 time rebuilding those from scratch.

## Team pass, night of 16 November 2026 (branch `v2.0.4`, worktree `C:\sc\v204`)

The owner's asks: TL;DR docs, game-dev research, "the car is all over the road", better scenery, manual hard to shift, general bugs, "the bridges go up and down". Five reviews ran in parallel (vehicle dynamics, GIS/bridges, UX/shifting, perception/scenery, QA); their findings and what landed:

| Ask | Root cause found | Landed |
|---|---|---|
| Car all over the road | Ackermann floor `max(0.001, tan(rack))`: the front wheels were never straight (+-0.028 deg, sign flipping 1380 times in a robot log) while the crown needs 0.015 deg; 1-3 cm lips swapped in their face normal (side push); the driver's gaze led every correction | Exact Ackermann (test `AckermannContinuous`); small steps keep the centre normal, kerbs tilt the tread envelope (max 30 deg); gaze leads only from 0.15 g |
| Hard to shift | Every shift needed A+RB within ~37 ms; a second press was lost; no rev-match; no stall help | Automatic clutch by default (press, lift, change, blip, release ~0.3 s), presses add up, no stall at a crawl; Gearbox row cycles 3 modes (`simcruise.Gearbox.AutoClutch`) |
| Bridges up and down | Per-station ground clamp copied DEM bumps into decks; same-class decks nearby gave single stations the wrong deck; junction joins pulled decks to the road below (and lifted it); approach smoothing left a step at abutments | One deck row per run; straight grade + at most one crest, never a sag (`FitSmoothDeck`); joins only within 3 m in height; 20 m approach fade; same fit for tiles without deck rows. Tests `BridgeDeckSmooth`, `BridgeOverRoad` |
| Scenery | Roads read as printed sheets; far tree shadows pop at 92 m; tiles pop in 0.5-1 km out; empty verges; power lines fetched but never drawn | Tar-snake crack sealant on older asphalt; far tree shadows on Ultra (`simcruise.Trees.FarShadows`). Open: wheel paths and joints (needs road UVs), tile fade-in, verge clumps |
| Bugs | `main` did not link (PR #19 dropped `ASimCruiseTraffic::IsEnabled`); launch fix 483c412 never merged; C8 1->3; roof ridge across the short side; Auto graphics re-benchmarks; AA cvar warning | All fixed |
| "I lost control easily" (17 Nov) | Tyres and steering match the real cars on the benches, but no car had stability control; the owner last drove the Corvette and the Raptor, which have it | ESC on the six cars that have it (van Zanten yaw/side-slip control, one-wheel braking, torque and regen cut), HUD light, pause menu switch; FMVSS 126 sine-with-dwell bench (all six pass with it, five spin without) |

Still open from the reviews: the helper's deck end heights depend on tile build order (`helper/road-grade.mjs` `endHeight` memo); `World.TerrainNeverOverRoad` is still a report (Doraville 60 points, worst 0.86 m); graphics quality applies on every D-pad step; the graphics persistence test changes the session's settings; crosswind is drag only; yaw is limited together with roll and pitch in the contact guard.
