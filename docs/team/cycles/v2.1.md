# OSDS 2.1 plan

**In short**
- Goal: fewer bugs (aim: none a player meets) and cars that perform, sound and adjust like the real ones.
- Two teams: **Physics and automotive** (research, standardize, improve the cars) and **QA** (play the game at random places, report, we fix, repeat).
- Graphics work only where it clearly beats what we have (list below).
- Google 3D map data cannot ship (licence); CARLA's MIT code and its CC-BY props can be used with care.

## Team 1: Physics and automotive

### Answers to the owner's questions (Miata audit, 2026-11-17)
- **Tyre:** the owner's car runs 205/50R15 200-treadwear tyres, a correct and good choice; size and gearing in the file are right. The grip level is an estimate (lateral peak 1.09, skidpad target 0.98 g). Tread pattern is not modelled, only grip.
- **"32 psi boost":** the NB has no turbo; 32 psi is a tyre pressure. The door placard says 26 psi front and rear. The game has no tyre pressure yet: 2.1 adds it.
- **Handbrake for drifting:** today a 900 N m on/off lock on each rear wheel, applied at once, and the auto clutch stays engaged, so a pull in gear fights the engine. Real NB: a cable lever to the rear calipers, used with the clutch down.
- **Shifting:** the auto clutch presses in about 55 ms, holds 40 ms, releases in 200 ms (up) or 250 ms (down), with a throttle blip on downshifts. A downshift that would over-rev is refused; a real gearbox lets you do it, so the refusal must say why on the HUD.
- **Rev counter:** works: the dash needle reads engine rpm (0.04 s lag), red from 7000.
- **Sound:** synthesized from wavetables tied to harmonic number, so the timbre moves with rpm like a sped-up recording; nearly flat loudness with rpm; overrun pops of a modified exhaust; identical cylinders; weak intake; muffled cabin filter.

### Car-data standard (every car)
Mass, CG height (from NHTSA's Static Stability Factor), inertias (NHTSA inertial database or its regressions); wheel and bar rates, roll centres, camber gain, anti-geometry, caster, steering ratio, Ackermann, dampers; tyre size, load index, speed rating, treadwear, pressure, ply type (TMeasy from Chrono's GuessPassCar70Par method, grip fitted to the skidpad); torque curve, gears, diff type, engine inertia; brake torques, parking-brake type; CdA and lift. Each value tagged with its source or "estimate". Benches every car passes: 0-60, 70-0, skidpad bands; ISO 4138, 7401, 3888-2, 7975; FMVSS 126 for cars with ESC. Today only the Miatas have roll centres and relaxation lengths; no car has anti-geometry, pressure or a parking-brake type.

**Owner's own car:** confirm brake rotor size, diff type, 5- or 6-speed (VIN or build data) and weigh the car; until then those stay estimates.

## Team 2: QA (loop)

1. Play at random places: `scripts\preflight.ps1 -Roads randomus -RandomCount 8` and `-Roads hard`, rotating cars (include the ESC cars and the Miata); plus a short hand-checked run of menus, tuning and the pause menu.
2. Report every failure with run, frame and place into `docs/release/bugs.md`.
3. Fix the top ones, rerun the same seed to confirm, then a new seed. Repeat.
4. Open from 2.0.4: Mackinac mid-span border throw; High Five level steps (probe vs real); Lombard robot; US-550 border jolt; driveway lips; the button-hint bar covers the last settings row (Throttle trigger).

## Graphics (only if better than what we have)
- Light bounced from sunlit walls into shaded streets, baked per tile on the CPU (0 ms GPU).
- Lane-aware road wear: wheel paths, oil strip, concrete joints (about 0.1 ms).
- Roadside objects where none are mapped: delineators, poles with wires, mailboxes, fences.
- Tree level-of-detail check (fade bands exist; confirm no hard cut).

## World data: Google Earth and CARLA
- **Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles:** needs a key and billing, no storing beyond cache headers, no deriving our own meshes from them, Google logo and credits on screen; melted photogrammetry at street level with no collision. Cannot ship. At most an optional "bring your own key" display layer later; not planned.
- **Better legal sources instead:** USGS 3DEP lidar (roof shapes, heights), state orthophotos at about 15 cm where public domain, city 3D models where open (e.g. NYC), Overture and Microsoft footprints (already used).
- **CARLA (UE5, 0.10):** code MIT, art CC-BY (separate content repo). Worth taking: the Traffic Manager design and OpenDRIVE lane/junction model (for when traffic returns), US traffic signs, signals and street furniture (credit each in CREDITS.md). Do not take: brand badges and names, anything from Epic marketplace packs, pedestrian models until cleared, partner content, the FBX SDK.
