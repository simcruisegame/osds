## Trees and buildings first (owner, 2026-11-14 23:45Z)

Owner's answer to "what looks the most fake": "the trees", "and buildings". In the world lane, trees and buildings are now equal first for the look, as two parallel pieces of work, directly after bridge clearance. The AI lane's wall library feeds the buildings. ### Trees (`World/SimCruiseTrees.cpp`)

Why they look fake: one mesh per kind stretched in X, Y and Z to the crown box, so the trunk thickens with the crown (:763; the measured crown base at :393 is not used); species by a lat/lon box and a coin flip per tree (:434-446, :755), and `helper/forest-form.mjs` (LANDFIRE EVT) is never called; one green per kind (:311-314, :75); flat, evenly lit crowns (mesh normals, :176); no wind (no world position offset on leaves, :290-350); nothing under 3 m (:382) and shrubs are tinted far crowns, 16 per cell (:634, :812); shadows end at the far ring (:623); a hard cut at 600 m (:557, :620); a full rebuild on the game thread on each canopy arrival and rebase (:664, :777-781); no contact darkening at the trunk foot (:682). The near rings already cross-fade (:51-68); onboarding row 11 "no fade" is out of date.

Order:
1. Colour per crown from the aerial photo at the crown, plus ±8 % hue and ±15 % brightness per tree; a darker crown interior (sphere normals, about 40 % darker at the centre); bark darker in the bottom 30 cm. About 1 day, under 0.1 ms.
2. Species from LANDFIRE EVT through the existing helper (USFS TreeMap later), in stands, not a coin flip. About 1.5 days, 0 ms.
3. No pop, no hitch: dither out over 540-600 m; shadows fade over 80-96 m; one tree component per tile, built off the game thread and swapped in. About 1.5 days, 0 ms.
4. Shape variants: about 24 trees grown offline with our own space-colonisation code (Runions et al. 2007), picked by kind, height-to-width ratio and crown base; stretch at most ±20 %; trunk at uniform scale; random flip. About 5 days, +0.05 ms.
5. Bushes and forest edges: canopy 0.5-3 m tall from the canopy map becomes bushes, and branches reach the ground at forest edges within 60 m of the road; a CC0 shrub asset is still to be found. About 4 days, 0.1-0.2 ms.
6. Wind: measure first (magician 0.15 ms; environment-artist 0.2-0.5 ms from shadow redraws); ship only within the 0.3 ms rule.

Proof: fixed-pose Ultra frames beside a licensed photo of the same place: Snake River Overlook WY (`lab/landscapes/wyoming/forest-form/`), Tail of the Dragon at Deals Gap, Lincoln NE street trees (`lab/holdout/views/lincoln-city-trees.jpg`), plus the random places. Pass: crown colour spread within ±30 % of the photo; a 5 km drive with no tree pop past 500 m and no game-thread hitch over 8 ms when tiles arrive; tree GPU p95 within the budget for each step.

### Buildings (`World/SimCruiseTileDetail.cpp`, `World/SimCruiseWorldResources.cpp`)

Why they look fake: every wall is flat paint, one of 16 colours plus one grain (Resources :572, TileDetail :176-189, :566); one window grid on every wall, sides, backs and garages too, windows flush with the wall (Resources :589-593, :615); no doors, and shop glass only on Main Street or mapped shops (TileDetail :840, :848-850); most places become flat boxes with a 0.5 m parapet, houses only with the place graph's recipe (:525, :556), a pitched roof only on a box-like footprint (:1148), fixed 0.35 m eaves with no fascia or gutter (:1150), and OSM `roof:shape` reaches the graph (`SimCruisePlaceGraph.cpp:527`) but not the builder; no ground contact: walls start 0.3 m under the lowest corner, no foundation or steps (:523, :532), and `helper/nsi-buildings.mjs:32-34` drops NSI construction type, foundation type and height, and year built.

Order:
1. Wall material from data: NSI building type and Overture facade material choose brick, lap siding, stucco or block; brick coursing (ASTM C216 modular, 3 courses per 8 in) and siding laps drawn in the shader. About 1.5 days, 0.05-0.1 ms.
2. Windows by wall role: back and side walls 0-40 % of the bays, garage walls none, paired windows on houses. About 1 day, 0 ms.
3. Doors as geometry: one recessed door on each street front (0.91 x 2.03 m, IRC R311.2), steps up to the NSI foundation height; shopfronts from shop tags. About 1.5 days, 0.05 ms.
4. Houses everywhere: NSI RES1 and RES3A are houses without the place graph; honour `roof:shape`; fascia and gutter within 80 m. About 1 day, 0.03 ms.
5. Foundation band from the NSI foundation height; ground height per wall, not the lowest corner. About 0.5 day, 0 ms.
6. The AI lane's wall library (SDXL outputs, cleared under the owner's licence rule of 2026-11-14 01:56Z; MatSynth items checked one by one, CC0 or CC BY with credit) replaces the shader materials near the car; sills and lintels within 80 m.

Proof: 20 buildings per place, each frame beside a licensed photo of the same building (Library of Congress, Highsmith, Commons): Detroit Woodward Ave (brick commercial), Welch WV McDowell St (brick Main Street, about 1910), Doraville GA Buford Hwy (strip malls, ranch houses), one random suburb chosen by seed from NSI RES1 tiles. Pass: wall type matches the photo on 80 % or more; every street front shows a door; no flat-box houses in the suburb; GPU p95 within 0.3 ms at Ultra by day and night.
