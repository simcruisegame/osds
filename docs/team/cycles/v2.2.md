# OSDS 2.2 plan

**In short**
- Goal: the most realistic free driving simulator: cars that drive like the real ones, 12 sports cars with real data, every major US race track, and a world whose surfaces read as photographs.
- Quality over speed: 2.2 ships when every team's "done" test passes and the owner says so.
- Method: measure first, change one thing, measure again against a real reference (a road test, a photo of the same place, a recording). Every claim in a commit or doc names its source or says "estimate".
- The coordinator (main session) owns this plan, QA, optimization, reviews and merges. Teams work on their own branches and open PRs; nobody merges their own PR.

## Teams

### 1. Race tracks (session "race-tracks")
Every NASCAR Cup track, Richmond, and the major road courses (Watkins Glen, Road America, Laguna Seca, Sebring, Road Atlanta, VIR, COTA, Mid-Ohio, Barber, Lime Rock, Sonoma, Indianapolis road course), very detailed:
- Real geometry from OpenStreetMap `highway=raceway`, real lengths checked against published figures.
- Banking from published figures per turn (Daytona 31 deg, Talladega 33, Bristol 24-28, ...), transitions, apron, pit road, start/finish line, walls with SAFER barriers and catch fences, kerbs on road courses, track-surface textures (grippy aggregate, rubbered-in racing line, seams).
- Menu: "Oval tracks" and "Road courses", real place names, no logos or series branding, a not-affiliated line.
- Done: each track's start resolves to the track; drive captures at five tracks match reference photos; a robot lap at three tracks without leaving the surface.

### 2. Cars and driving (session "cars-driving")
Twelve sports cars, each with real data and hyper-realistic handling:
- The 12: Miata NB, Corvette C8, 1965 Mustang, Mini Cooper S, Camaro IROC-Z (have); Dodge Charger R/T 2020, Audi TT 3.2 quattro 8J (CARLA models); Porsche 911 Carrera S 992, Toyota GR86, Nissan Z, Honda Civic Type R FL5, Ford Mustang GT S550 (Sketchfab models, downloaded with the owner's sign-in). Research files: `lab/cars/*-research.md`. Note: CARLA's "Mustang" is a 1966 GT, not a 2016.
- Driving realism (vehicle dynamics list): relaxation length that shortens at saturation; real differential types (open, clutch LSD with preload, Torsen, eLSD, Haldex); roll centres and anti-dive/anti-squat for every car; steering compliance from aligning torque; staggered tyre sizes per axle; the Camaro full-throttle spin found and fixed at its cause.
- Done: every car passes CarTrack (0-60, 70-0, skidpad within band), StaticLoad, LiftOff, StepSteer (ISO 4138 understeer within a published band), HandbrakeTurn, and SineDwell for ESC cars; a drive capture of each car matched against photos.

### 3. Tuning (session "tuning")
The owner wants to adjust power and chassis now; upgrade parts come later.
- Horsepower and torque (shown in hp and lb-ft, with the curve), rev limit, final drive and gear ratios, springs, dampers (bump and rebound), anti-roll bars, ride height, camber, toe, caster where adjustable, brake bias and brake force, differential preload and ramp, tyre pressure, ESC and traction control levels.
- Every value stays in a safe range around the real car's; the menu shows the effect (roll per g, 0-60 estimate, top speed) before you drive; pad-only.
- Done: every row works on the pad, is saved per car, and moves the matching bench number the right way (a test per row).

### 4. Hyper-real surfaces (session "surfaces-research"): research and development
New, efficient ways to make surfaces read as photographs through shading, not polygons: visual illusions.
- Research first: survey the state of the art (parallax occlusion and relief mapping, tri-planar detail, detail and macro variation, wetness and dust layers, micro-occlusion, specular anti-aliasing, curvature and edge wear from data, procedural weathering, neural texture synthesis, stochastic texturing that hides tiling), measure each idea's GPU cost on the owner's RTX 2070 Super Max-Q, and pick by picture gained per millisecond.
- Targets: concrete and asphalt (aggregate, cracks, patches, oil, rubber), curbs and gutters, building walls (edges that are not perfectly square, chipped corners, rain streaks, grime by height), grass (3D near the car, parallax "hologram" grass further out), dirt and gravel shoulders.
- Done: side-by-side frames against photos of the same kind of surface, a measured cost per effect, and the effects on in every tile at High and Ultra.

### 5. Engine sound (session "engine-sound")
Cars must sound like the real ones.
- Real recordings, licensed (CC0 or CC BY) or recorded by the owner (a how-to for his Miata: steady rpm steps, on and off throttle, inside and outside), played as rpm-and-load crossfaded loops the way racing games do, with the synth kept as a fallback.
- Turbo (spool, whoosh, blow-off), V6 and distinct V8s, the Model 3's motor whine, cabin and exterior mixes, a volume menu.
- Done: each car's sound against its reference recording (spectrogram orders and loudness steps), and a blind A/B where the owner picks the real one less than 60 % of the time.

### Coordinator (main session): QA, optimization, integration
- Nightly random-place drives at Ultra on the owner's laptop (preflight `-Roads randomus,hard`), every failure logged in `docs/release/bugs.md` and fixed at its cause.
- Optimization: frame time at High (target 45+ fps in the seat at 1080p on the owner's laptop) and Ultra; download size (lighter CARLA grass, capped textures); hitches on tile load.
- Open from 2.1.x: blocky rear-view mirror; Camaro hood camera; tile-border jolts; Mackinac throw.

## Rules for every team
- Work on your own branch from `main`; open a PR; the coordinator reviews and merges.
- One Unreal editor or GPU job at a time on the owner's PC: use the locks in `scripts/EditorLock.ps1` (preflight, check and release scripts take them).
- Names: the project is OSDS (OSDS.uproject, Source/OSDS, classes AOsds/FOsds/UOsds, cvars osds.*, options -Osds...). A branch older than the rename runs `scripts/rename-to-osds.py` before merging.
- Licences: OpenStreetMap ODbL, CARLA and Sketchfab models CC BY 4.0 with credits, no brand logos or badges, no Google 3D data.
- Docs in plain English; each team adds its part to the 2.2 section of CHANGELOG.md and `docs/release/notes/2.2.0.md`.
