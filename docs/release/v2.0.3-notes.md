**OSDS (Open Source Driving Simulator) is a free, open-source driving simulator for Windows: type any US address and drive the real roads there, built live from OpenStreetMap, USGS elevation and aerial photographs. Realistic car physics, traffic, a GPS, and wheel, Xbox controller or keyboard. Made in Unreal Engine 5.** Website: https://simcruisegame.github.io/osds/

## Download

- **Installer:** `OSDS-2.0.3-windows-x64-setup.exe` (per-user or all users; Start menu entry; uninstaller). It updates an installed 2.0.0, 2.0.1 or 2.0.2. SmartScreen may warn because the build is not code-signed: **More info > Run anyway**.
- **Portable:** `OSDS-2.0.3-windows-x64.zip`.
- **Checksums:** `OSDS-2.0.3-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, a DirectX 12 graphics card with 6 GB of video memory and an internet connection for the map data. Full requirements: [docs/install.md](https://github.com/simcruisegame/osds/blob/v2.0.3/docs/install.md).

## SimCruise is now OSDS

From 2.0.3 the game is called **OSDS, the Open Source Driving Simulator**, with a new logo and app icon: an "O" broken open by a road. It was named SimCruise until November 2026. The game window is titled OSDS. The installer updates an installed SimCruise 2.0.x in place and moves the Start menu entry from SimCruise to OSDS. Your saves, settings and map cache stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SimCruise`, and the program file is still `SimCruise.exe`. The repository moved to [github.com/simcruisegame/osds](https://github.com/simcruisegame/osds) (the old address redirects) and the website to [simcruisegame.github.io/osds](https://simcruisegame.github.io/osds/).

## New in 2.0.3

It fixes the cockpit, makes every menu work with an Xbox controller, adds fine detail to the ground, trees and buildings, and makes the roadside, the traffic and the start of a drive look and feel more like the real place.

**Fine detail** <!-- FINE DETAIL: the world lane gives the steps that are in 2.0.3; write one line each for the ground, the trees and the buildings, true to the build -->

**In the car**
- **The steering wheel turns true.** The cockpit wheel turns about its real centre, at the ratio you set in Tune car, and no faster than real hands can turn it. Each car turns its real number of turns from lock to lock: the Miata 2.6, the Corvette about 2.5 and the 1965 Mustang 3.73. A USB wheel uses the same steering column.
- **The gauges work in all three cars.** The Miata's five needles move, the Corvette's digital cluster shows the speed and a live rev arc, and the Mustang's ribbon speedometer, fuel and temperature gauges work.
- **The Miata has ABS,** so it steers while you brake hard, and the brake trigger is gentler in the first half of its travel.
- **The Corvette turns in** at speed instead of running wide, and the Mustang's driver sits 6 cm higher, as in the real car.
- **Speed in mph or km/h** (pause menu, Settings, Speed units). The GPS distances and the map scale follow your choice.

**Menus and controls**
- **An Xbox controller reaches every menu,** the page tabs at the top included. Up from the first row goes onto the tabs, LB and RB change the page from anywhere, B goes back one screen and Menu closes everything. The road atlas buttons and the Where to? search (with a pad keyboard) work with the D-pad.
- **The button hints follow the device you used last:** pad buttons when you use the pad, keys when you use the keyboard or mouse.

**The picture**
- **A sharper, steadier picture.** The picture is sharpened after the upscale, motion blur is shorter and follows the real frame rate, and VSync is on by default, so no tear line rolls through the picture.
- **Smooth frames again on the Tail of the Dragon,** and the trees beside the road no longer vanish for a frame.
- **The cabin no longer flips from black to bright** when you drive in and out of shade, and a black dash stays black under trees.
- **Sharper aerial photographs without the AI model.** When the detail model is not there, the ground photographs get a classic sharpening.

**The world**
- **Rural roadsides like real ones:** past the shoulder the ground falls into a ditch, then climbs into a cut or drops to the valley side.
- **Roofs take their colour from the aerial photograph,** and fields and lawns no longer glow lime green.
- **Guard rails** where OpenStreetMap maps them, as well as where the ground drops away.
- **The real climate for each place** (Köppen-Geiger 1991-2020): the season, the grass and the trees follow it. Tennessee lawns turn tan in November and Miami stays green.
- **Fewer road steps where two roads meet,** no bridge wall across a road that meets a bridge at its own level, and no spikes in the land to the horizon.

**Traffic** <!-- TRAFFIC: check the three lines against the traffic lane's final CHANGELOG entries -->
- **No turning round in the road.** Traffic cars no longer turn round in the middle of a road.
- **A full gap for you.** A traffic car waits for a full gap before it pulls out or cuts into your lane.
- **Freeway traffic stays on the freeway.** Motorway cars no longer drive across streets at grade.

**Starting and streaming**
- **The car starts on a road that goes on:** a public road, in the traffic lane, with 800 m or more of road ahead, checked on the ground before the car goes there.
- **No freeze at the start or when the first traffic car appears.** Car models are built while the start tiles load.
- **Tiles load ahead at highway speed.** The game loads the tiles on the line to where the car will be first; at 130 km/h each tile is ready before you get there. If a tile is late, the car cannot fall through.

All changes, with the measurements: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/simcruisegame/osds/blob/v2.0.3/CHANGELOG.md).

## Known issues in 2.0.3

- At a few junctions a small lip stays where two roads meet (seen at Welch, West Virginia and Doraville, Georgia).
- In direct sun the Miata's dashboard can show small shadow marks.

All open bugs: [docs/release/bugs.md](https://github.com/simcruisegame/osds/blob/v2.0.3/docs/release/bugs.md). Please report a problem in [Issues](https://github.com/simcruisegame/osds/issues), with the place and the car.
