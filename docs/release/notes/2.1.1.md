# OSDS 2.1.1: full release notes

The short version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

**In short:** real ray-traced lighting on High and Ultra, graphics presets retuned (High for laptops like an RTX 2070 Max-Q, Ultra for stronger PCs), road wear from CARLA's decals, rounded curbs and street corners, and fixes from the owner's first 2.1 drive (cars parked across side streets, a black band on the horizon).

## Look
- **Ray-traced lighting (High and Ultra).** Lumen now traces the world with the GPU's ray tracing. The game builds its roads and buildings while you drive, and outside the editor those meshes had no distance fields, so the lighting could not see them: cabins looked clay-grey, cars floated, streets were flat. Now the cabin is shadowed, cars sit on the road with contact shadows and streets have depth. GPUs without ray tracing keep the previous lighting.
- **Presets retuned.** High is tuned to an RTX 2070 Super Max-Q laptop (75 % render scale, about 45-55 fps; Auto picks it there). Ultra is for stronger PCs (100 %, Epic global illumination, denser roadside plants, mirrors every frame). Measured at 1080p on the RTX 2070 Super Max-Q: High 53 fps from the seat and 64 in the chase view; Ultra 33 and 47.
- **Road wear (CARLA decals, CC BY 4.0).** Alligator cracking, crack sealing, stains and long cracks are stamped along every road by rule, more on older stretches, none on gravel. They stay put; nothing is placed by hand.
- **Rounded curbs and street corners.** Curbs have a fillet at the gutter, a battered face and a rounded nose. Where two streets meet, the curbs are joined by a curved curb return (up to 5 m radius) with the corner paved.
- **Walls are not perfect planes.** Render, concrete and masonry undulate slightly, so raking light shows them as built.
- **Overcast skies are no longer flattened.** The shadow lift that suits harsh sun eases off as the cloud closes in.

## Fixes
- Cars were parked across the mouths of side streets and inside junctions (the owner's Miami Beach screenshot). Parking now keeps 9 m clear of any road joining the curb.
- A black band could show on the horizon where distant map tiles were not built yet. A far ground under the world now shows hazy distant land instead.
- Shader errors with ray tracing on (custom material code now samples textures in a way ray-tracing shaders accept; CARLA plants stay out of the ray-traced scene).

## Known issues
- The rear-view mirror image is still blocky (under investigation; the mirror capture's resolution is lost somewhere other than screen percentage, mip choice or temporal anti-aliasing).
- The Camaro's hood camera sits inside its hood bulge.
- The 2.1 known issues remain (the Camaro spins if floored, its model has no cabin, an occasional tile-border jolt).
- The random-places drive test was not completed for this patch (stopped for the release at the owner's call).
