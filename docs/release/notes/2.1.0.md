# OSDS 2.1.0: full release notes

The short version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

**In short:** two new cars (Mini Cooper S, 1987 Camaro IROC-Z), a handbrake you can drift with, tunable tyre pressure, a Miata that sounds like one, and art from the CARLA simulator: traffic-signal poles, roadside grass and bushes by climate, brick and weathered concrete. Trees pick their species from the aerial photo.

## Cars and driving

- **Two new cars.**
  - **MINI Cooper S (F56, 2015):** 189 hp 2.0 turbo, 6-speed manual, front drive, stability control. The model is the CARLA Team's Mini (CC BY 4.0), exported from Unreal to glTF; badges, lettering and tyre markings are removed, the dash is black matte as on the real car. Bench (Car and Driver's test): 0-60 6.74 s with rollout (6.6), 70-0 176.7 ft (172), skidpad 0.901 g (0.91).
  - **Chevrolet Camaro IROC-Z 5.7 (1987):** L98 V8, 700R4 four-speed automatic, no ABS, no traction control, foot parking brake, a V8 voice with the GM firing order. Model: "1985 Chevrolet Camaro IROC-Z" by Tony (Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0, made in Blender). Bench (Road & Track, October 1986): 70-0 202.6 ft (208, derived), skidpad 0.867 g (0.85). Open item: the bench floors the throttle from rest and spins the rear tyres to 33 mph (8.6 s against 6.8 s); ease on and the launch is right. The model has no cabin, so the seat view shows the chase camera, as with the CR-V and Odyssey.
  - Data and sources: `lab/cars/mini-research.md`, `lab/cars/camaro-research.md`; files written by `scripts/osds-cars/make_new_cars.py`. Every bench passes for both: CarTrack, StaticLoad, LiftOff, StepSteer, HandbrakeTurn, and the FMVSS 126 sine-dwell for the Mini.
  - A more detailed Camaro on Sketchfab was turned down: its textures looked taken from a video game.
- **A handbrake you can drift with.** It pulls in over about 0.15 s like a lever, locks the rears, presses the automatic clutch for you and holds stability control off while pulled. Cars with an electric parking brake (Corvette, Model 3) brake all four wheels gently instead, as the real ones do (`parking_brake_type`). Bench `OSDS.Vehicle.HandbrakeTurn.<car>`: rears lock within 0.2 s, yaw over 100 deg/s, engine running.
- **Tyre pressure,** front and rear, from the door sticker (the Miata 26/26 psi), tunable 18-40 psi in the tuning menu. Grip follows the pressure the way measured tyres do. Test `OSDS.Vehicle.Model.TyrePressure`.
- **The Miata sounds like one.** The four-cylinder voice now has fixed exhaust and intake resonances (180 Hz and 1.9 kHz, stronger under load), loudness that rises with rpm, small cylinder-to-cylinder differences, and no overrun pops on stock cars. The idle is judged against a recording of a real NB.

## World

- **CARLA art (CC BY 4.0, CARLA Team).**
  - **Traffic-signal poles, bases and mast arms** at signalised junctions (8, 5.6 or 3.9 m arms by road width); OSDS's own signal heads hang from them.
  - **Roadside grass and bushes**, 83 CARLA models picked per climate (north, south, wet, dry, tropic): lush up north, sparse shrubs in the desert, along rural road edges only (never on a sidewalk or in town). `osds.World.CarlaVerges 0` turns them off.
  - **Brick and weathered concrete** on walls, as a ratio over each wall's own colour.
  - Only the CARLA models the game places are packaged (a cook list made with the world materials), not the 21 GB repository.
- **Trees pick their species from the aerial photo.** The region gives the share of conifers; each crown's own look moves its odds (darker or more slender crowns lean pine). The low deserts (Phoenix, Tucson, Las Vegas) no longer get pines. Test `OSDS.World.Trees.ConiferShare`.
- **Divided roads can be a start again** (Ward Parkway, Kansas City): the start picker cut the median as a junction.

## QA

The test robot drove 23 runs at random and hard US places with the Mini, the Camaro, the lowered Miata and the Corvette before the owner called the release: 21 passed. Known issues from it:

- **Camaro on the Mackinac Bridge:** at full throttle the robot spun it (rear slip 65 deg) and left the road; the Mini and the Miata passed the same run. The IROC-Z has 434 N m, no traction control and no stability control, as the real car; a smoother throttle and a check of its rear grip are next.
- **A jolt at a map-tile border** in a random suburb (Mini), the known tile-seam issue.
