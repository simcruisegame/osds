# OSDS 2.2.0 (unreleased)

## Engine sound

- **Each car has its own engine.** The Odyssey and the F-150 Raptor now have a V6, with the growl of alternating banks. The Mustang 289, the Corvette, the Camaro and the Grand Cherokee SRT8 each have their own V8 firing order and exhaust colour: a deep, soft 289, a bright C8, a muted L98. The Mini and the CR-V stay inline fours.
- **Turbo:** the Mini, the CR-V and the twin-turbo Raptor whine as boost builds, breathe in, and sigh when you lift.
- **The Model 3** now makes sound: its motor's whine and gear mesh, louder as it pulls and as it regenerates.
- **Recorded engines:** the game can play an engine recorded at steady speeds, as racing games do. [Recording your car](../../recording-your-car.md) explains how to record a car on a phone, and a script turns the recordings into the game's loops. No car ships with recordings yet. The first will be a real 2001 Miata.
- **Volume:** Settings has Master, Engine and World volume rows (the World row covers tyres, road, wind and traffic).
- How it works and how it is tested: [docs/engine-sound.md](../../engine-sound.md).
