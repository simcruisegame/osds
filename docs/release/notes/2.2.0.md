# OSDS 2.2.0: full release notes (in progress)

The short version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Tuning

The tuning menu (F4 or D-pad right, standing still) now covers power and the whole chassis, on six pages (LB / RB). Every value stays in a safe range around the real car's, a row shows only if the real car has that part, and every row is saved per car. The full table is in [docs/controls.md](../../controls.md#tune-your-car).

- **Engine.** Peak torque in lb-ft scales the whole torque curve; peak power in hp shapes the curve above the torque peak (as a cam and tune do), solved so the curve's peak is the hp you set. The rev limit moves the fuel cut, and the automatic's change-up points follow it. Electric cars tune motor torque and power.
- **Gearbox.** Final drive and each gear's ratio (±20 %, each gear kept taller than the one before).
- **Springs and dampers.** Springs front and rear, bump and rebound damping front and rear (the damper's force in each direction, on both the ratio-law and the dyno-curve dampers), anti-roll bars, and ride height front and rear (rake).
- **Alignment.** Camber, toe and caster where the real car has the adjustment (`data/tuning/adjustable.json`, with the source or "estimate" per car; front toe on every car). Toe is now per axle in the sim.
- **Brakes and differential.** Brake bias and brake force; limited-slip lock on power and on the coast for cars with a limited-slip differential. Preload follows when the clutch-type LSD model lands.
- **Tyres and aids.** Tyre pressures; stability control On / Sport / Off and traction control On / Sport / Off on cars that have them. Sport doubles the stability control's dead bands and side-slip limit and lets the traction control allow more wheelspin (estimates of the makers' sport modes).
- **The effect before you drive.** The torque (amber) and power (lime) curves over rpm with the real car's in grey; peak torque and power and their rpm; an estimated 0-60 mph (a quick straight-line model; its change is applied to the real car's published time, so a stock car reads its magazine figure) and top speed; body roll per g.
- **Tests.** One automation test per row (`OSDS.Tuning.Row.*`) runs the matching bench on the stock and the tuned car and checks the number moves the way the real car's would: more torque, a quicker 0-60 on CarTrack; more power, a higher top speed; a stiffer front bar, less yaw on the ISO 7401 step steer (more understeer); more bump damping, a shallower compression after a push; Sport stability control acting less in the FMVSS 126 sine with dwell; and so on. `OSDS.Tuning.StockIsReal` checks the stock setup drives exactly as the real car.
- **2.1 setups** (one damping %, one ride height, a torque %) load into the new rows.
