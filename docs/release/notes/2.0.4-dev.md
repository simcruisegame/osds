# OSDS 2.0.4 (in development): full release notes

The short version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

- **Fix: manual shifts accept a simultaneous clutch press** — a shift request now waits for the physics-step clutch ramp before checking engagement, while a request without the clutch still reports the refusal after a short buffer. Input-tape physics replay follows the same path. The reverse-at-speed and over-rev guards remain in force.
- **Bridge decks keep their mapped profile** — the tile parser now reads the helper's per-station bridge heights, and road ribbons preserve those heights through profile smoothing and uncontracted tile seams. The seam no longer pins a raised deck to bare earth beneath it; terrain clearance is still enforced. Older tiles without exported deck profiles keep the existing fallback.
- **Mapped house roof forms are used** — supported `roof:shape` facts now select flat, gabled, or hipped procedural roofs instead of being ignored in favor of a random house-roof guess. Unmapped or unsupported shapes keep the procedural choice.
- Fix: traffic follows the traffic again. A car turned round wherever its road ended with no way on, and a road also ends at the edge of a tile not built yet: cars made U-turns in the middle of the road near the edge of the built world. They now turn round only at a true dead end (the road ends more than 30 m inside its tile). And the shorter gap a kept-waiting car accepts is for other traffic only: the player always gets the full 4.5 s (a left turn) or 5 s (a stop sign). A road that ends on a tile's boundary never counts as a dead end. And traffic keeps off a motorway span the map data draws crossing a street at grade (I-75 under Woodward in Detroit, its bridge not raised: a row of freeway cars drove across Woodward in front of the player); a ramp joining a motorway is no crossing, nor a street on a bridge that clears the motorway by 4.9 m; a mapped bridge drawn at the motorway's own level, like Woodward's, still counts. A road close by in plan but more than 3 m above or below is no longer offered as a turn.
