# OSDS 2.0.4 (in development): full release notes

The short version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../../CHANGELOG.md).

- **Fix: manual shifts accept a simultaneous clutch press** — a shift request now waits for the physics-step clutch ramp before checking engagement, while a request without the clutch still reports the refusal after a short buffer. Input-tape physics replay follows the same path. The reverse-at-speed and over-rev guards remain in force.
- **Bridge decks keep their mapped profile** — the tile parser now reads the helper's per-station bridge heights, and road ribbons preserve those heights through profile smoothing and uncontracted tile seams. The seam no longer pins a raised deck to bare earth beneath it; terrain clearance is still enforced. Older tiles without exported deck profiles keep the existing fallback.
- **Mapped house roof forms are used** — supported `roof:shape` facts now select flat, gabled, or hipped procedural roofs instead of being ignored in favor of a random house-roof guess. Unmapped or unsupported shapes keep the procedural choice.
- Fix: traffic follows the traffic again. A car turned round wherever its road ended with no way on, and a road also ends at the edge of a tile not built yet: cars made U-turns in the middle of the road near the edge of the built world. They now turn round only at a true dead end (the road ends more than 30 m inside its tile). And the shorter gap a kept-waiting car accepts is for other traffic only: the player always gets the full 4.5 s (a left turn) or 5 s (a stop sign). A road that ends on a tile's boundary never counts as a dead end. And traffic keeps off a motorway span the map data draws crossing a street at grade (I-75 under Woodward in Detroit, its bridge not raised: a row of freeway cars drove across Woodward in front of the player); a ramp joining a motorway is no crossing, nor a street on a bridge that clears the motorway by 4.9 m; a mapped bridge drawn at the motorway's own level, like Woodward's, still counts. A road close by in plan but more than 3 m above or below is no longer offered as a turn.

## Night of 16 November 2026 (team pass)

### Driving
- **The car holds its lane.** The front wheels could never point exactly straight: the Ackermann step floored tan(rack) at 0.001, so any rack off zero put both wheels at least 0.028 deg off centre, while a road's 2 % crown needs only about 0.015 deg. The car hunted left and right of the angle it needed (in a 13 November robot log, 3548 of 3548 centred-stick frames above 8 m/s had the wheels at +-0.03 deg, flipping sign 1380 times). The wheel angles now use the exact form, continuous through straight ahead. Test `SimCruise.Vehicle.Model.AckermannContinuous`.
- **Small road lips no longer steer the car.** A step of 1-3 cm under a tyre's shoulder (a tile seam, a junction lip) used to swap in that step's face normal (up to 70 deg), pushing the tyre sideways by load x sin(tilt). Up to 3 cm the tyre now rolls over it on its tread (the contact rises, the road under the centre sets the normal); a real kerb (over 3 cm) tilts the tread's envelope by atan(step / half tread), at most 30 deg.
- **A calmer driver's view.** The eyes lead into corners (0.15 g and up, full at 0.5 g, over 0.8 s), not into every lane correction: the old lead turned the view more and sooner than the car turned, which invites over-correction.
- **Manual gearbox with an automatic clutch (new default).** Tap RB/LB (E/Q): the game presses the clutch, lifts the throttle, changes gear, blips the throttle on a downshift to match the engine to the lower gear, and lets the clutch out (about 0.3 s). Two taps drop two gears (a second press used to be lost). At a crawl it holds the clutch so the engine never stalls; pull away on the throttle alone. Pause menu > Gearbox cycles Automatic, Manual (automatic clutch), Manual (your clutch). `simcruise.Gearbox.AutoClutch`, saved as `auto_clutch`. Tests `SimCruise.Vehicle.Model.AutoClutch*`.
- **Hard launches change up.** The automatic's upshift guard now reads the output shaft, so a Mustang or Corvette with spinning rears no longer sits on its fuel cut in 1st (bench: at most 0.5 s on the fuel cut at full pedal, `SimCruise.Vehicle.ShiftDrive.*`).
- **One gear at a time.** No second upshift within 0.7 s of the last (the Corvette went from 1st straight to 3rd on a hard launch).

### World
- **Bridges are flat across.** Each bridge run is now one smooth deck: a straight grade between its two end heights plus at most one crest (never a sag), fitted to the helper's per-station heights with residuals clipped to +-3 m. The stations' own heights carried noise that made decks go up and down: a neighbouring deck's height (twin or crossing decks of the same class; a run now keeps to one deck row), the elevation model's bumps (a per-station ground clamp copied them into the deck; removed), and chain splits at ramps that dropped a point to the valley floor. Where an approach meets a deck, the small step left by the profile smoothing fades out over 20 m. Tests `SimCruise.World.BridgeDeckSmooth`, `SimCruise.World.BridgeOverRoad`.
- **A road under a bridge is not a junction.** Junction joins and side cuts only join surfaces within 3 m of each other in height: a deck over a road was pulled down to it within 15 m (and the road below lifted to the deck).
- **Gable roofs run along the house.** The ridge follows the long side of the footprint's box; the longest single edge could be on the short side (a long wall mapped in two pieces).
- **Sealed cracks on older asphalt** ("tar snakes"): thin meandering lines of black sealant on some stretches, filtered by pixel footprint so they do not shimmer.

### Fixes
- **The game builds again.** A merge (PR #19) dropped `ASimCruiseTraffic::IsEnabled()`, so `main` did not link.
- Graphics "Auto" in the pause menu picks once per session (a GPU missing from the tier table ran Unreal's synthetic benchmark on the game thread each time).
- No "r.AntiAliasingMethod ... ignored" warning on every launch (it is set in DefaultEngine.ini).
- "T (B) for manual" said the wrong button: it is T or the right-stick click.

### Docs
- README and CHANGELOG cut to a TL;DR; every release's full notes moved to `docs/release/notes/<version>.md`.

## Second pass (17 November 2026)

- **You feel and see a shift.** A manual shift is a 45 ms tick on the pad (up on the right motor, down on the left) and the HUD's gear grows and turns white for a quarter second. A refused shift buzzes twice, and the message says what to do: "Too fast for 2nd: below … mph (… km/h)", with the speed that gear can take. The same refusal twice in a row is shown twice (it was shown once).
- **Bridge ends agree in every tile** (map helper). A tile that could not measure a bridge's far end took the near end's height (a flat deck in the first tile built, a sloped one in the next, a kink at the seam); it now takes the remembered height, else the elevation model at that end. A joint inside a bridge (a ramp joining on the deck) is no longer taken for an end on the ground under it: it lies on the deck between the true ends (1/distance along the deck). New offline test `lab/check-bridge-decks.mjs`.
- **Stability control (ESC).** The owner lost control easily; the handling benches showed the tyres and steering behave like the real cars, but no car had stability control, which every US car has had since model year 2012 (FMVSS 126). The Corvette, Model 3, F-150 Raptor, Grand Cherokee, Odyssey and CR-V now have it (`stability_control` in the car file); the 2001 Miata and the 1965 Mustang do not, as the real ones did not. It follows van Zanten's ESP (SAE 2000-01-1633): the yaw rate the steering asks for (a single-track model with the car's own understeer gradient, `esc_understeer_deg_per_g`, measured on our 80 km/h step steer; held within 1.1 mu g / v; lagged 0.06 s) against the car's own, and the side slip at the centre of mass against 8 deg slow, 4 deg at speed. Past dead bands (oversteer 0.05 rad/s + 15 %, understeer 0.10 rad/s + 30 %) it brakes the outer front against oversteer or the inner rear against understeer (through ABS), cuts engine torque, and takes an electric car's regeneration off in a slide. The HUD's ESC light flashes while it acts. Pause menu > Your car > Stability control (`simcruise.Assists.StabilityControl`, saved as `stability_control`).
  - New bench `SimCruise.Vehicle.SineDwell.<car>`, the FMVSS 126 sine with dwell (80 km/h, 0.7 Hz, 0.5 s dwell, 6.5 x the 0.3 g steer): with ESC on every car keeps at most 35 % of its peak yaw rate 1.0 s after the steering ends and 20 % at 1.75 s, and still moves 1.83 m sideways (all six: 2.5-3.7 m sideways, at most 23 % of the yaw left at 1.0 s). With it off the CR-V, Raptor, Grand Cherokee, Odyssey and Model 3 spin (side slip 48-87 deg); the Corvette wobbles (47 % left at 1.0 s).
  - LiftOff: a 0.7 g brake in a bend spun the Model 3 (45 deg) and the Odyssey (9.6 deg); now 5.2 and 4.6 deg. ESC never acts on a steady circle, after a lift or light brake up to 0.5 g, or in a 0.4 g step steer (gates). `SimCruise.Vehicle.EscOff.*`: switched off it never acts.
- **No ground over the road at Doraville.** A road off a bridge joins a deck only within 1 m in height (a ramp deck 2.7 m over I-285 pulled the motorway's edge up to it and tilted the lane), and the ground just past a road's closed end is held at the end's height. The recorded Doraville place went from 60 points of ground over the road (worst 0.86 m) to 1 (0.54 m, a motorway's end on a tile seam). `SimCruise.World.TerrainNeverOverRoad` is now a gate: at most one point per place, none over 1 m, all on tile seams.
