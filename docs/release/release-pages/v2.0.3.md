**A world that looks more real.** Free, open-source driving on real US roads, built live from open map data. Windows 10/11.

## Download
- **Installer:** `SimCruise-2.0.3-windows-x64-setup.exe` (installs the Microsoft runtimes it needs; updates 2.0.x in place). SmartScreen may warn (not code-signed): **More info > Run anyway**.
- **Portable:** `SimCruise-2.0.3-windows-x64.zip`. **Checksums:** `SimCruise-2.0.3-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new
- Trees take their colour from the aerial photo; walls are brick, siding, block, stone, panels or metal; windows and doors follow the wall.
- Each wheel knows its surface (asphalt, concrete, gravel, grass, kerb); you hear and feel it.
- Engines without a turbo lose power at altitude; the automatic shifts by road speed.
- Every menu works with the Xbox controller alone.
- About 2 ms of GPU back on Ultra; sharper HUD digits.
- Moving traffic and aircraft are off for now; parked cars stay.
- The installer works on a clean PC.

## Known issues
- Some motorway bridges meet the road below at the same level; small lips where driveways join roads.
- Facades and street lights repeat; Xbox impulse triggers do not rumble.
