**Fixes from the first play test.** Free, open-source driving on real US roads, built live from open map data. Windows 10/11.

## Download
- **Installer:** `SimCruise-2.0.1-windows-x64-setup.exe` (updates 2.0.0 in place). SmartScreen may warn (not code-signed): **More info > Run anyway**.
- **Portable:** `SimCruise-2.0.1-windows-x64.zip`. **Checksums:** `SimCruise-2.0.1-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new
- A less bright, more natural picture and a better field of view.
- Settings work with the pad; your own stick and trigger settings.
- The Miata's rear brakes no longer lock first; engine braking includes pumping loss.
- AI models left out of the download (smaller install).
