**A GPS and a new look.** Free, open-source driving on real US roads, built live from open map data. Windows 10/11.

## Download
- **Installer:** `SimCruise-2.0.2-windows-x64-setup.exe` (updates 2.0.0 or 2.0.1 in place). SmartScreen may warn (not code-signed): **More info > Run anyway**.
- **Portable:** `SimCruise-2.0.2-windows-x64.zip`. **Checksums:** `SimCruise-2.0.2-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new
- GPS routes on the real roads, with a turn sign and a round map on the HUD.
- New pause menu: plan a route, famous roads, "Where to?".
- Seat & view menu, hood and far-chase cameras, look around with the right stick.
- Fixes: a car off the road no longer explodes; no road step at tile borders.
