OSDS 2.0.4 is a free, open-source driving simulator for Windows: drive the real roads anywhere in the US, made in Unreal Engine 5.

**Driving you can trust.**

## Download
- **Installer:** `OSDS-2.0.4-windows-x64-setup.exe` (installs the Microsoft runtimes it needs; updates 2.0.x in place). SmartScreen may warn (not code-signed): **More info > Run anyway**.
- **Portable:** `OSDS-2.0.4-windows-x64.zip`. **Checksums:** `OSDS-2.0.4-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new
- **The car holds its lane.** The front wheels point dead straight now; small road lips no longer push the tyres sideways; the driver's view stops swinging with every correction.
- **Stability control (ESC)** on the cars that have it in real life (Corvette, Model 3, F-150 Raptor, Grand Cherokee, Odyssey, CR-V), with the ESC light. All six pass the US legal ESC test. Turn it off in Pause menu > Your car.
- **Manual with an automatic clutch** (new default): tap RB/LB to shift. A rumble tick and a gear flash with each shift.
- **Bridges are flat across**, and no ground pokes through the road.
- **Buildings from real data**: real heights, building use and storeys, mapped roof shapes, wall colours and materials.
- **Looks:** lifted shadows, no pop when the sharper aerial photo arrives, sealed cracks on older asphalt.
- **Bug hunt across the US** with the test robot, and the bugs it found fixed (the Mackinac Bridge start, bridge decks at map-block cuts).
- Simpler docs: every page starts with a plain "In short".

## Known issues
- The Mackinac Bridge can still throw the car at one map-tile border.
- Stacked interchanges (the High Five in Dallas) may show steps between levels; small lips where some driveways join roads.
- Moving traffic is off for now; parked cars stay.
