Engine sound recordings used by OSDS
====================================

OSDS ships no third-party audio. From the recordings below it ships only numbers
measured from them (engine-order levels in data/sound/reference/*.json), made with
scripts/engine-sound/cut_loops.py analyze. Licences checked on each Freesound page
on 18 November 2026.
