CARLA simulator content
=======================

OSDS uses art from the CARLA simulator's content repository
(https://bitbucket.org/carla-simulator/carla-content, branch ue5-dev),
by the CARLA Team (Computer Vision Center, Barcelona, and contributors),
https://carla.org.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

What OSDS uses, and how it is changed:
- Traffic-signal poles, bases and mast arms (Static/Pole), placed by OSDS's own rules at
  signalised junctions found in OpenStreetMap; OSDS's own signal heads hang from them.
- Roadside grass and bushes (Static/Vegetation), chosen per climate band and placed along
  rural road edges by OSDS (data/carla/props.json).
- Brick and concrete-weathering textures (Static/GenericMaterials/Facade), used as a
  ratio over each wall's own colour in OSDS's building shader.
- The Mini Cooper model (Static/Car/4Wheeled/MiniCooper), exported from Unreal to glTF
  with baked materials; maker badges and tyre lettering painted out or removed.

Only the models the game places are packaged. No brand names or logos are taken.
Car names and brands are trademarks of their owners and are not licensed.
