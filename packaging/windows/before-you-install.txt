OSDS: read this before you install
===================================

OSDS (Open Source Driving Simulator) was named SimCruise until
November 2026. An update keeps your settings and map cache.

1. INTERNET
   Ocean Drive, Miami Beach is in this installer. You can drive there
   with no internet connection.
   All other places need an internet connection. The game downloads the
   map, the terrain and the photos of a place when you go there.

2. MICROSOFT PARTS
   The game needs two free parts from Microsoft:
   - the Visual C++ 2015-2022 runtime (x64). Without it, the game
     cannot start.
   - GameInput. Without it, racing wheels do not work.
   If your PC does not have them, this installer installs them at the
   end. They are for the whole PC, so Windows asks for your permission.
   Click "Yes". If you click "No", the game asks again when it starts.

3. THE PC THAT YOU NEED
   - Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
   - A graphics card for DirectX 12, with 6 GB of video memory or more.
   - 16 GB of memory (RAM).
   - 3 GB of free disk space for the game, and up to 2 GB more for
     the map cache and the downloaded photos.
   - Keyboard, Xbox controller, or a USB racing wheel.

   IMPORTANT: We tested the game on one PC only: Windows 11, an NVIDIA
   GeForce RTX 2070 Super with Max-Q Design (8 GB), an 8-core Intel
   Core i9-10885H and 32 GB of memory. We did not test AMD or Intel
   graphics cards, other NVIDIA cards, or Windows 10. The values above
   are our estimate. If the game does not work on your PC, tell us:
   https://github.com/simcruisegame/simcruise/issues

4. UNINSTALL
   The uninstaller deletes the game, the map cache, the downloaded
   photos and the logs. It asks you before it deletes your settings and
   screenshots.
